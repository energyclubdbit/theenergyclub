Club Mentor

Dr. Yogesh S. Padiya

Dr. Yogesh S. Padiya is a Certified Energy Auditor and Ph.D. graduate in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Bombay with over 25 years of experience in academia, energy management, and industrial consulting. He specializes in energy efficiency, waste heat recovery, Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) systems, heat pumps, industrial pollution control, and sustainable engineering solutions. Currently, he serves as an Energy Consultant and Professor at Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Mumbai, where he has been working since 2018. His responsibilities include promoting research and innovation, conducting industrial energy audits, identifying cost-saving opportunities, and developing advanced energy-efficient technologies. He also continues his work as an Energy Auditor at Eco Energy Solution, providing complete solutions for reducing industrial energy costs and implementing sustainable projects.
